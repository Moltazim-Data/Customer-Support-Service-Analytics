# Insight Playbook

Treat these as questions to investigate, not findings until calculated and verified.

1. SLA risk: which priority/category groups combine high volume with lower attainment?
2. Channel mix: do response times differ by channel?
3. Backlog health: is the queue concentrated in urgent or aging tickets?
4. Team workload: are assignments uneven? Account for tier and case complexity.
5. Customer experience: does CSAT vary by category, channel, priority, or resolution duration?
6. Trend changes: are there unusual demand spikes?

For each verified insight, document the metric, segment, period, comparison, business impact, recommended action, owner, timing, and follow-up KPI. Avoid treating correlation as causation, missing CSAT as a low score, or SLA outcomes with missing results as breaches.
